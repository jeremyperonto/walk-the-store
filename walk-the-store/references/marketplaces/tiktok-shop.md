# TikTok Shop — listing rules and functional best practices

Sources verified: 2026-07-26. If a finding hinges on a hard limit below and this date is >90 days old — or the live page contradicts it — re-fetch the source before citing, use what the source says today, and flag the drift.

## Hard rules (official)

- **Title:** concise and accurate; front-load product type + defining attribute in the first 30–40 characters — mobile cuts the rest.
- **Images:** up to 9 per listing, 1:1 ratio, minimum 800×800px, full color (black-and-white not accepted), no pixelation, no text or watermarks beyond the product's own branding.
- **Listing quality tiers:** every listing is scored Poor / Fair / Good, and the tier gates search and recommendation distribution. Listings with fewer than 5 quality images can't reach Good.
- **AI-generated imagery requires a visible "AI-generated" disclosure label.**

## Ranking and content-quality mechanics

- The feed is the search path and video is the detail page: discovery runs through creator content and shop videos far more than keyword search. The product card must survive the cut from an autoplaying video to a purchase decision in one screen.
- Creator/affiliate content volume plays the role review count plays elsewhere — it's the trust proxy the persona actually sees.
- The in-video price anchor must match the product card; any gap between what the creator said and what the card shows is an instant abandonment.
- Listing tier compounds: Good-tier content earns distribution, which earns creator pickup, which earns more distribution.

## Expert practice (beyond the docs)

- Build the image set for the feed, not the shelf: first image legible at feed-card size, benefit text on secondary images (allowed there — main image rules are stricter), before/after or in-use shots that mirror the video content that sells the product.
- Ship a shop video on every listing that matters; card + video listings convert the feed's intent while it's still hot.
- Seed affiliate samples deliberately (open collaboration + targeted invites) — affiliate commissions and sample flow are merchandising levers here, and the cost stack that comes with them belongs in any economics finding.
- Watch the compliance edge: TikTok delists aggressively for image-policy violations and title spam; a delisted week costs more than a Good-tier push earns.

## Walk checks specific to TikTok Shop

- Walk the feed as the persona (interests set accordingly): does the product appear organically? Through whose videos?
- Product card at feed size: image legibility, price vs. the in-video anchor, review stars vs. creator-content volume.
- Listing tier (if the owner has Seller Center access) as the internal metric for the Measure-it section.
- Shop page coherence: does the catalog look like one brand or a liquidation bin? The persona clicks through more here than anywhere.

## Mode-2 fetchability (observed 2026-07-26)

PDPs and profiles return "Security Check" or login shells. The sanctioned mode-2 search path: `shop.tiktok.com/us/k/<query>` — server-rendered SEO keyword pages with full ranked product cards (price, sold count, rating, badges, PDP links). Caveat in every report: the SEO grid is not the in-app feed; feed rank and video presence stay unverifiable without a browser.
