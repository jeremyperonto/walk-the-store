# Walmart Marketplace — listing rules and functional best practices

Sources verified: 2026-07-26. If a finding hinges on a hard limit below and this date is >90 days old — or the live page contradicts it — re-fetch the source before citing, use what the source says today, and flag the drift.

## Hard rules (official)

- **Title:** clear and descriptive, under 200 characters (aim well under — ~150), title case, no all-caps words, no promotional phrases ("Free Shipping," "Best Seller," "Sale," "#1"), no special characters or HTML. Formula: Brand + Defining Quality + Item Name + Pack Count + Size/Color.
- **Primary image:** pure white background (RGB 255,255,255), actual product photography — stock photos are not permitted. No condition descriptions in images ("grade A," "refurbished").
- **Image specs:** JPEG/PNG/BMP, 1:1 square, recommended 2200×2200px (minimum 1500×1500 for zoom). Walmart recommends at least four images per listing.
- **Product details policy:** the Marketplace Learn portal's Product Detail Page guides are the canonical spec for every content field.

## Ranking and content-quality mechanics

- **Attribute/spec completeness is the ranking lever.** Walmart's search and Listing Quality scoring lean on filled item specs harder than any other major marketplace — audit attribute fill before touching copy.
- Walmart surfaces a **Listing Quality score** to sellers (content & discoverability, offer, ratings & reviews); it's the native measurement surface for playbook fixes.
- Price competitiveness gates visibility (items can be delisted or buried when beaten meaningfully elsewhere, including by Walmart's own shelf).
- Fulfillment speed (WFS / TwoDay tags) carries trust weight the way Prime does on Amazon.

## Expert practice (beyond the docs)

- Front-load the first 50–75 title characters — Walmart's grid and mobile truncate early.
- Fill every recommended attribute, not just required ones; each empty attribute is a filter the product can't survive.
- Rich media (comparison charts, feature blocks) closes the gap in categories where 1P listings set the content bar.
- Reviews syndication: Walmart accepts review syndication from brand sites via approved partners — the fastest route past an empty reviews block.

## Walk checks specific to Walmart

- Does the product survive the category's obvious filters? (The attribute-gap check matters most here.)
- Compare against Walmart's own 1P listing of the same/similar item — the persona sees both, and 1P sets the content ceiling.
- Listing Quality score (if the owner has Seller Center access) as the internal metric for the Measure-it section.
- Pro Seller badge presence; delivery tag vs. neighbors.

## Mode-2 fetchability (observed 2026-07-26)

All surfaces — search, browse, product, reviews, business.walmart.com mirror — return a "Robot or human?" CAPTCHA to fetch tools. Mode-2 walks run entirely on search-index snippets; say so per item. Selection order proxy: external search restricted to walmart.com, labeled.
