# Roadmap

Walk the Store ships as a release series — quality bar fixed, cadence aggressive. Inclusion bar for any marketplace: third-party vendors own their own merchandising and can win through content updates.

## Release series

- **v1.3 — marketplace playbooks** (in review): per-marketplace rules files for Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Etsy, TikTok Shop — official documentation linked with verification dates, expert practice, marketplace-specific walk checks, and a freshness rule that re-fetches sources before stale limits get cited.
- **v1.4 — examples library:** real walks (with permission), published as the standard for what a report should look like; per-persona prompt packs (nursing student, boutique owner, gift buyer).
- **v1.5 — evaluation round:** delivery marketplaces — DoorDash and Uber Eats merchants own their menus, photos, and descriptions through merchant portals, which meets the inclusion bar with a very different walk anatomy (feed, photos, modifiers, ratings). Also evaluated: Target Plus (meets the bar, invite-only), Best Buy Marketplace, Wayfair, Faire.
- **Ongoing, every release:** recrawl official sources and refresh verification dates; ship user feature requests with changelog credit.

## The family

Walk the Store is the first of a set of marketplace-operator skills, all distributed through the [marketplace-skills plugin marketplace](https://github.com/jeremyperonto/claude-plugins). The walk finds what's costing conversions; the skills behind it prove and price the fixes. WTS matures through this series before the family expands.
